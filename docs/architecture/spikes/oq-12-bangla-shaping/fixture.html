4 · Matra reordering — vowel signs that move

ি is stored AFTER the consonant and must render BEFORE it. ো and ৌ are split matras: one part before, one part after. This is reordering, not substitution — a renderer that only does ligatures still fails here.

7 · Wrapping in a narrow column

A long Bangla name in a report-card-width cell. Clusters must never break mid-conjunct, and matras must stay attached to their base.

Narrow, roomy leading মোহাম্মদ শাহরিয়ার রহমান চৌধুরী Narrow, tight leading (line-height 1.0) মোহাম্মদ শাহরিয়ার রহমান চৌধুরী

The red cell tests matra/reph clipping: at line-height 1.0 the reph and ঊর্ধ্ব matras can be cut off by the line box. If the red cell clips and the green does not, the fix is per-script line-height, not a font change.
